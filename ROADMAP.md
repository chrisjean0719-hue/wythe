# Roadmap

Wythe plans a running, stack, or Flemish bond wall over HTTP, can leave a rectangular opening, can turn one end, and counts its bricks and mortar. This is the fourth of six updates. About 38 hours of later work stay after those six.

## Still in the six updates

### Elevation file (update 5)

Return the elevation as an SVG.

### Compare (update 6)

Compare two walls by brick count and mortar length.

## After the sixth update

These stay after the six updates. They are about 38 hours.

### Pier (4 hours)

Add a square pier at one end and count the bricks in each pier course.

### Coping (3 hours)

Add a coping course of a chosen thickness and include it in the height.

### Color pattern (4 hours)

Paint stretchers from a short list of brick colors and repeat the pattern along the course.

### Cut list (4 hours)

List every closer and any other cut, with the size to mark on the brick.

### Mortar volume (4 hours)

Turn the joint length and a chosen joint depth into litres of mortar.

### Foundation (3 hours)

Add a foundation course below the first brick course and report its length.

### Weep holes (3 hours)

Place weep holes on a chosen course at a chosen spacing and skip the bricks they replace.

### Height in millimetres (3 hours)

Accept a wall height and choose the course count that lands closest to it.

### Print sheet (3 hours)

Add a print stylesheet so the elevation and the counts fit on one page.

### Loose length (4 hours)

Accept a length in millimetres that is not a whole number of modules, and list the extra cut.

### Gallery (3 hours)

Show three sample walls and let the page open one of them.

An elevation file is the next piece. The API can turn one end, and it does not yet return an SVG.
